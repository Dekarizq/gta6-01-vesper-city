Vesper City: Neon Rain — a rain-soaked open-world crime game in the spirit of GTA 6, built for make.fun's "Make GTA 6" bounty ($1,666 in USDC). Play as Mara Voss, getaway driver, through 10 story missions in a neon night city under a 7-day hurricane countdown: drive and steal cars, outrun the police (wanted level 0-5, roadblocks, helicopter), fight on foot, and follow the HUD and minimap. Raw WebGL, no libraries, no network requests — fully offline static files.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. Controls: WASD/arrows drive & walk, E enter/steal car, F/click shoot, Space handbrake, Shift sprint/boost, M map, P pause, R restart mission.
